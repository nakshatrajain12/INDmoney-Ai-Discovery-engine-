### 4.3 Tensions inside the primary evidence

1. **Where is the payment gap?** P00 recalls no notification; P03 got one that lacked a reason. These point to different fixes (delivery vs content) and the notification logs would settle it.
2. **Worry versus action.** Everyone who worried about returns continued; the only stop (P02) was about money. Red-portfolio worry did not predict stopping in this set.
3. **Outside help.** It seems to reassure rather than push (P05), yet P00 and P04 manage without it.
4. **Cross-selling.** Absent for P00, mild for P04, situational for P01.
5. **Pause difficulty.** P00 reports no difficulty pausing or modifying; P02 was unsure what pause does. P02's confusion is simulated and not evidence of a product flaw.

### 4.4 What the primary research adds that the engine did not surface
- Contribution size and pay-date alignment as a protective factor (S5).
- Cash-flow stops and the "pause then forget" pattern (P02); the engine has no flexibility or restart theme.
- An actionable explanation of a system event (failure, pause), which is distinct from performance information.

## 5. Limitations
- Five of six perspectives are simulated; none is a prevalence estimate or independent validation.
- P00's expectations, first-mutual-fund status and exact checking frequency are not established.
- Invented details in the simulated files (jobs, amounts, timings) are flagged in each file's Grounding table.
- One platform comparison case (SIM-P05, Groww).
- No interview covers a user who stopped for performance reasons, a user who left within the first month, or a platform migration.
