# P01: At-risk continuer

## Metadata
- Participant ID: P01
- Perspective / sampling reason: first-time investor, SIP still active, felt like stopping around months 3-6
- First mutual-fund investor: Yes
- First SIP start: about 5 months ago
- Platform: INDmoney
- Current status: Active
- Tenure band: months 3-6
- Hypothesis tested: H1 (expectation vs experience). Competing explanation: worry may not convert to churn.

## Behavioural timeline
| Moment | What happened | Thought / feeling | Next action |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Before starting | Saw a colleague mention SIPs; read a few articles | Expected "something visible" by month 3 | Started a ₹2,500/month SIP |
| Setup / first debit | Smooth; first debit went through | Relieved | Opened the app daily for two weeks |
| Months 1-3 | Value hovered near invested amount | Mildly impatient | Compared the figure with a bank FD in their head |
| Months 3-6 | Return figure turned slightly negative after a market dip | Anxious; "is this normal?" | Googled; read a forum thread; asked a colleague |
| Most recent uncertainty | Considered pausing for a month | Torn | Decided to wait until the next instalment |
| Current | Active, checks weekly | Cautious | Continues, uncertain about horizon |

## Interview record (condensed)
**Context.** "I'd only had a savings account and an FD before. I started because a colleague said small amounts add up." Chose ₹2,500 because it "wouldn't hurt." Expected to see growth "by the third month, at least."

**First months.** Setup was fine. First two weeks: opened the app "every day." Later weekly. Looks at the current value and the percentage figure, "I saw XIRR but I didn't know what it meant."

**Critical incident.** After a dip the screen showed a small minus. "I thought, did I pick a wrong fund?" Searched online; found several answers that disagreed. A colleague said "ignore it, it's early." "What stopped me was that I'd only put in about ₹12,500 so the loss in rupees was tiny. If it were bigger I might have stopped."

**Workarounds.** Google, one forum thread, a colleague. Trusted the colleague most "because he's been investing for years." Found the app's own screen "just numbers."

**Payment.** No failures.

**Needs.** "Tell me if this is normal for five months. Not advice, just what usually happens." Avoid: "pushing me to buy other things when I'm already worried."

## Synthesis
- Triggering event: first negative return figure in months 3-5
- Main friction: not knowing whether the figure was normal
- Root cause (hypothesis): expectation set by FD comparison and a visible-growth expectation; no in-app reference for early-tenure fluctuation
- Emotional response: anxious, then uncertain
- Behavioural response: searched externally; delayed the decision; continued
- What helped continuation: small rupee loss; peer reassurance; no payment problem
- Unmet need (own words): "Tell me if this is normal for five months."
- Strongest quote: "I didn't know if minus two percent at five months was normal."
- Alternative explanation: continued because of low stakes, not because of understanding; the same worry at a larger ticket size might end differently

# P02: Paused SIP

## Metadata
- Participant ID: P02
- Perspective / sampling reason: first-time investor who actually paused within the first 12 months
- First mutual-fund investor: Yes
- First SIP start: about 9 months ago
- Platform: INDmoney
- Current status: Paused since about month 7
- Tenure band: months 6-12
- Hypothesis tested: what actually drives a stop. Competing explanation: cash flow and lost habit, not returns.

## Behavioural timeline
| Moment | What happened | Thought / feeling | Next action |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Before starting | Wanted a "savings habit" beyond a bank deposit | Hopeful | Started ₹5,000/month |
| Months 1-3 | Instalments fine | Satisfied | Checked monthly |
| Months 3-6 | Value slightly up, then flat | Neutral, not worried | Did nothing |
| Month 7 | Freelance income dropped for two months | Stressed about rent | Wanted to pause the SIP |
| Pausing | Unsure how pause differs from cancel; worried the mandate would stay active | Anxious about fees | Paused after reading help text |
| Since | Meant to restart after one month; has not | Mild guilt | Plans to restart "when income is steady" |

## Interview record (condensed)
**Context.** Variable income. Chose ₹5,000 "because it sounded like a proper start," later thought it was too high. Had no emergency fund.

**First months.** "I wasn't watching the returns much. Flat didn't bother me."

**Critical incident.** Income fell. "The money was needed for rent. The SIP wasn't the problem, I was." Looked for how to reduce; wasn't sure whether reduce, pause or cancel was best. "I wasn't sure if pausing kept my mandate on or if I'd get charged." Paused. Intended one month.

**What happened next.** "It's been three months. No reminder asked me to restart. I just forgot, and then it felt awkward."

**Workarounds.** Searched the help section; asked a friend. No forum use.

**Payment.** One bank notification about the mandate during the pause; unclear if related.

**Needs.** "A clear explanation of what pause does. A nudge to resume. And suggest a lower amount instead of a pause."

## Synthesis
- Triggering event: income drop around month 7
- Main friction: unclear difference between pause, reduce and cancel; no prompt to resume
- Root cause (hypothesis): amount set too high for variable income; pause with no re-engagement path
- Emotional response: stress, then mild guilt
- Behavioural response: paused; did not restart
- What helped continuation before the pause: low attention to returns; habit
- Unmet need (own words): "Tell me what pause does, and remind me to restart."
- Strongest quote: "The SIP wasn't the problem, I was out of money that month."
- Alternative explanation: a one-month pause that becomes permanent is inertia; the starting amount may have been the real issue

## Grounding
| Element | Status | Basis |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Stopping for cash-flow reasons rather than returns | Supported (theme) | P00 (would pause if pocket money fell, pack §16.2 Q10) |
| Confusion about pause vs cancel | Observed | Direct participant account (contrasts with P00; public reviews mention cancellation problems in other flows) |
| No restart prompt | Observed | Direct participant account |
| Amount "too high" for income | Observed | Direct participant account (contrasts with P00 who chose a small amount specifically to avoid this) |
| Freelance job, ₹5,000, timings | Observed | Direct participant account |

# P03: Payment-friction, recovered

## Metadata
- Participant ID: P03
- Perspective / sampling reason: failed debit early in the journey; recovered and continued
- First mutual-fund investor: Yes
- First SIP start: about 3 months ago
- Platform: INDmoney
- Current status: Active, one failed instalment
- Tenure band: months 1-3
- Hypothesis tested: H2 (payment friction). Competing explanation: a notification may exist but not be seen or understood.

## Behavioural timeline
| Moment | What happened | Thought / feeling | Next action |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Setup | Set up UPI autopay for ₹1,500/month | Easy | Forgot about it |
| Month 2 | Instalment date fell on a day the UPI daily limit was used up | Unaware | None |
| After failure | Got a notification: "instalment failed" | Confused: no reason shown | Checked the bank app, found the limit issue |
| Resolution | Topped up, retried manually | Annoyed | Wondered if the SIP was still live |
| Month 3 | Next instalment went through | Relieved | Continued |

## Interview record (condensed)
**Context.** Wanted a monthly habit. ₹1,500 "to be safe."

**First debit.** Fine.

**Critical incident.** "I did get a notification, it said failed. It didn't say why or what I should do. I only found the limit problem because I checked my bank." Wasn't sure whether the SIP "was cancelled or just that month." Worried about bank charges; none appeared. "If that had cost me a penalty I'd have been angry."

**Trust.** "It made me less sure the app would tell me things." Considered stopping "for a day." Didn't.

**Workarounds.** Bank app; a search for UPI limits. No forum.

**Payment clarity.** "Reason and next step" would have fixed it. "I'd also like a reminder the day before."

**Needs.** Failure reason, next step, whether the SIP stays active, one-tap retry.

## Synthesis
- Triggering event: failed debit in month 2
- Main friction: notification without reason or next step
- Root cause (hypothesis): daily UPI limit conflict; app message not actionable
- Emotional response: confusion, mild annoyance, dent in trust
- Behavioural response: investigated via bank; manual retry; continued
- What helped continuation: no bank penalty; next instalment succeeded; low amount
- Unmet need (own words): "Tell me why it failed and what to do."
- Strongest quote: "It told me it failed. It didn't tell me why or what to do."
- Alternative explanation: they continued because no penalty occurred; with a charge the outcome could differ

## Grounding
| Element | Status | Basis |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Failed debit with weak communication | Supported (theme) | P00 (failed debit, no notification recalled, ₹300 penalty, pack §16.2 Q6-Q7); public reviews on payment/withdrawal |
| Notification **present** but not actionable | Observed | Direct participant account (contrasts with P00 who recalled no notification) |
| No bank penalty in this case | Observed | Direct participant account (contrasts with P00 who did get a penalty) |

| ₹1,500, dates, outcome | Observed | Direct participant account |

# P05: External-validation seeker / expectation mismatch

> **Platform: Groww (comparison case).** Do **not** attribute anything here to INDmoney.

## Metadata
- Participant ID: P05
- Perspective / sampling reason: relied on creators and online communities; expectation mismatch around months 3-6
- First mutual-fund investor: Yes
- First SIP start: about 5 months ago
- Platform: Groww (not INDmoney)
- Current status: Active; considered pausing, did not
- Tenure band: months 3-6
- Hypothesis tested: H3 (going outside the app). Competing explanation: outside help may reassure and retain rather than push toward stopping.

## Behavioural timeline
| Moment | What happened | Thought / feeling | Next action |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Before starting | Watched several creator videos promising high long-term returns | Excited | Started two small-/mid-cap SIPs |
| Months 1-3 | Returns fluctuated; slightly positive | Optimistic | Checked daily |
| Months 3-5 | Sideways; slightly negative | Disappointed vs expectation of fast growth | Watched "market crash" videos |
| Month 5 | Conflicting views: one creator said stop, another said continue | Confused | Asked on a forum and a friend |
| Resolution | Forum replies explained time horizons; a friend said hold | Calmer | Kept the SIP, lowered the check frequency |

## Interview record (condensed)
**Context.** "I started after watching a few videos. They talked about big annual returns."

**Expectation.** "I thought a few months would show something."

**Critical incident.** Return turned slightly negative. "That didn't match what I'd been told." Looked at the app's chart and "couldn't tell if it was normal." Went to videos and a forum. "Half said stop, half said wait."

**What decided it.** "A forum reply explained that early months of a SIP are mostly flat, and a friend said he'd stayed through worse. I stayed."

**Trust.** Trusted the friend most; "a creator can say anything."

**Payment.** None.

**Needs.** "Tell me what a normal first year looks like before I start." Avoid: "showing only the best-performing funds."

## Synthesis
- Triggering event: negative return after expecting quick growth
- Main friction: mismatch between creator-set expectations and early reality; conflicting outside advice
- Root cause (hypothesis): expectations set externally with no neutral reference at or after sign-up
- Emotional response: disappointment, confusion, then calm
- Behavioural response: sought videos, forum and friend; continued
- What helped continuation: a clear time-horizon explanation from a forum and a trusted friend
- Unmet need (own words): "What a normal first year looks like."
- Strongest quote: "Half the internet said stop and half said wait."
- Alternative explanation: outside sources both created the doubt and resolved it; the net effect on retention is unclear
