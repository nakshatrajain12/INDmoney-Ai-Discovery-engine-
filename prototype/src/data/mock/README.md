# Mock data: persona fixtures

**Everything here is illustrative demo data.** Fictional schemes (Northstar, Harbor, Summit, Evergreen, Beacon, Meridian, Cedar, Orchid), a fictional "Demo Bank ••4012", invented NAVs, invented investors. No NAV is real market data; no figure comes from the research survey; no customer exists. Every record carries `isMock: true` and `dataLabel: "DEMO DATA"`, and every valuation says `navSource: "ILLUSTRATIVE_DEMO"`.

The fixtures are generated by `builders.ts` from small specs (`fixtures.ts`), so units, values and totals are consistent by construction, and then checked independently by `validate.ts` and the tests.

## Conventions
- Money is **integer paise**. NAV and units are **integers scaled by 10,000** (`navE4`, `unitsE4`).
- Dates are ISO calendar dates; the fixture "today" is **2026-11-08** (`FIXTURE_AS_OF`). The effective date is: explicit `asOf`, then the demo clock, then the fixture date.
- SIP **status is never stored**; it is derived by `src/data/status.ts` (precedence: CANCELLED > PAUSED / RESTART_PENDING > PAYMENT_FAILED > ACTIVE).
- `declared.totalInvestedPaise` and `lastSuccessfulInstalmentDate` are totals "as declared by the data source". The validator checks them against the instalments; the Phase 2 engine recomputes them and must agree.
- Each persona declares its expected status, return band and **intentional data anomalies**. The validator detects anomalies independently and requires the two to match, so a data problem can never be accidental.
- Return bands here are a fixture sanity check (flat within ±0.5%, small negative down to −5%); the formal bands arrive in Phase 2.

## Adding or changing a persona
1. Add a `persona(...)` entry in `fixtures.ts` with its expected status, return band and any intentional anomalies.
2. Run `npm test`. The validator names every inconsistency. If it reports an undeclared anomaly, fix the data or declare it.
3. If the persona is needed by `docs/evaluation-framework.md`, keep the names in sync (a test reads that section).
4. Never use a real fund, bank, person or contact detail; a test fails if you do.

## Assumptions and limitations (documented for the owner's final fixture review, D-15)

Status: **provisionally accepted by the owner; final fixture sign-off pending the owner's inspection of this file.** Fixtures have been preserved unchanged.

1. **NAVs are invented.** Every NAV path is illustrative (`navSource: "ILLUSTRATIVE_DEMO"`). None is, or may be shown as, real market data. The Phase 2 engine does not read NAV, units or NAV history at all; it uses instalment cash and the valuation's current value (tested by scrambling all NAV data).
2. **Values are derived from invented NAVs**, so every return, absolute figure and XIRR is a demonstration of the calculation, not a statement about any fund.
3. **`MOCK_` payment reason codes are simulated.** The real provider's code list is unknown. `PSP_ERR_77` and the no-reason case are deliberately unmapped.
4. **Fictional entities only:** schemes, "Demo Bank ••4012", investors. Every record has `isMock: true` and `dataLabel: "DEMO DATA"`.
5. **Instalments settle on their due date** unless a failure was later recovered (then `settledOn` is the recovery date). Real settlement lags are not modelled.
6. **The 30 personas are designed test cases, not a sample of users.** They carry no prevalence or research meaning and none comes from the survey.
7. **`xirr-fails`** is a 3-day daily SIP with +2.5%. Annualised, the rate is above the engine's +10,000% search bound, so XIRR is unavailable. Phase 2 proves the reason: with the bound opened to 1e15 the solver finds a root (so the failure is the bound, not missing data).
8. **`inconsistent-xirr`** has a provider XIRR (+4.12%) that contradicts the negative return; the engine recomputes (-8.37%) and flags a mismatch rather than adopting the provider figure.
9. **`missing-instalments-gap`** declares totals from the recorded instalments only, so the engine's invested figure agrees with the declared total; the missing instalments are flagged by the fixture validator (`SCHEDULE_GAP`), not by the returns engine. A later phase decides how a gap lowers confidence.
10. **`restart-pending`** is paused through 19 Nov 2026 and restarts on 20 Nov, 12 days after the fixture date of 8 Nov and has an opted-in reminder. Under D-11 it is RESTART_PENDING because of the date, never because of the reminder.
